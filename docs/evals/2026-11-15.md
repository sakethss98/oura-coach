## Notes

- This run uses the defaults approved on 2026-11-15: the agent at low reasoning effort, the classifier at minimal,
  and the two checks reworded after the first run (rubric_changes.md).
- Routing is the classifier's own pick. With a goal program running, code also adds goal_program to a message
  routed to training, so a question about today's program session gets the program's tools: 226 of 228 final routes
  include a labeled category.
- Part of the logging and training gains is the two reworded checks, not the effort change.
- Lower at low effort: goal_program (trials passed 60% → 50%; the "says what isn't measured" checks) and
  trends_patterns (73% → 60%; most of it the "which days were not logged" check, still to be calibrated).
- Known misses, left for structured output in M5: G-2 (the coach's line next to a fueling plan is generic) and
  G-7 (asks a new question instead of saying what it knows; 3 of 3 passed in this run).
- Cost of this run: $0.77 coach + $1.99 judge = $2.76.
